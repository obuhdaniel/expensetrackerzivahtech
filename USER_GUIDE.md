## What KippaMe does

KippaMe helps you keep a personal record of the money you spend. You can add expenses, see your current-month total, understand your largest categories, and request a short AI-generated summary. Money is currently displayed in Nigerian naira (₦).

## Create your account

1. Open KippaMe and select **Get started**.
2. Enter your full name, email address, date of birth, and a password of at least eight characters.
3. Enter the password again and submit the form.
4. If you receive a confirmation email, follow its link before signing in.

Use an email address you can access and never share your password.

## Sign in and out

Select **Log in**, enter your registered email and password, and submit the form. To sign out, open the side menu if you are on a phone and select **Log out** near the bottom. Always sign out on shared devices.

## Understand the dashboard

The Dashboard shows the current month's information:

- **Spent this month** — the total and number of recorded expenses
- **Top category** — the category with the highest total
- **AI summary** — an optional plain-language overview
- **Spending by category** — how the total is divided
- **Expenses** — individual records for the current month

When search or a category filter is active, totals and breakdowns reflect the matching results.

## Add an expense

1. Select **Add expense** on the Dashboard.
2. Add a short description such as “Groceries”.
3. Enter the amount using numbers; for example, `2500` means ₦2,500.
4. Choose the closest category.
5. Choose the date the money was spent. Future dates are not accepted.
6. Select **Save expense**.

The record appears on the Dashboard when its date is in the current month. Use consistent descriptions and categories for clearer reports.

## Find and organize expenses

Use the search box to find words in expense descriptions. Use the category list to show one category, and select **Clear filters** to restore the complete list. Select a table heading to change sorting, and use the page controls when more results are available.

## Edit an expense

1. Find the expense on the Dashboard.
2. Choose its **Edit** action.
3. Correct the description, amount, category, or date.
4. Save your changes.

Totals and category breakdowns update automatically.

## Delete an expense

Find the expense, choose **Delete**, review the confirmation, and confirm. Deletion cannot be undone inside KippaMe. Add the record again if it was removed accidentally.

## Generate an AI summary

1. Add at least one expense dated in the current month.
2. Find the **AI summary** card.
3. Select **Generate summary** and wait briefly.

The summary uses recorded expenses to highlight totals and patterns. It may compare the previous month when those records exist. Automated summaries may be inaccurate and are not financial advice. If generation fails, try later; the service or its usage quota may be temporarily unavailable.

## History and profile

Select **History** to see up to 100 recent expenses, newest first. Select **Profile** to see your name, email, date of birth, and membership date. Profile details cannot currently be edited inside KippaMe.

## Common questions

### Why is an expense missing from the Dashboard?

The Dashboard only shows the current calendar month. Check the date, clear active filters, or look on the History page.

### Why did my total change after searching?

Totals reflect active search and category filters. Select **Clear filters** to restore the full current-month total.

### Can I add my own category?

Not currently. Choose the closest category or **Other**.

### Can I recover a deleted expense or export records?

There is no restore or export feature yet. Re-enter an accidentally deleted expense manually.

## Protect your account

- Use a unique password.
- Never send your password to anyone.
- Sign out on public or shared computers.
- Check amounts and dates before saving.
- Treat AI summaries as informational, not professional financial advice.

## Need help?

Refresh the page and try again. If a problem continues, note what you were doing and any message shown, then contact the person or team that provided your KippaMe access.
